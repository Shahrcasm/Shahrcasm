## 👋 About me

I'm an engineering undergrad majoring in **Computer Science & Engineering** at the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering, **North South University**.
I like work that goes deep: from page faults in an OS to world models in reinforcement learning to apps that people actually use.

- 🔭 **Currently researching** test-time adaptation for the **TD-MPC2** world model under dynamics shift
- 🧠 **Interested in** reinforcement learning, neural networks, operating systems and mobile development
- 🤝 **Open to** collaborating on research projects and open-source tools
- 📍 **Based in** Dhaka, Bangladesh
